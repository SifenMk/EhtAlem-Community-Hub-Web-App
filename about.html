About EhtAlem

EhtAlem (እህት አለም) means "Sister World" in Amharic. We're building a comprehensive ecosystem that empowers Ethiopian women entrepreneurs to succeed through sisterhood support, AI-powered mentorship, and culturally authentic resources.

Our Story

EhtAlem was born from a simple observation: Ethiopian women entrepreneurs, especially those without family support, face unique challenges that generic business platforms don't address. We saw talented women like Selam struggling alone, without the sisterhood support system that could make all the difference.

Our founder, inspired by her own journey as a solo entrepreneur, realized that success isn't just about business skills—it's about having a community that understands your cultural context, celebrates your victories, and supports you through challenges.

Today, EhtAlem serves as a digital sisterhood where Ethiopian women can connect, learn, and grow together. We're not just a platform; we're a movement that's reshaping the entrepreneurial landscape for women across Ethiopia.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do and shape the experience we create for our community.

Safety First

Creating secure, judgment-free spaces where women can be vulnerable, ask questions, and share without fear.

Inclusivity

Welcoming women from all backgrounds, regions, and experience levels. Every voice matters and deserves to be heard.

Innovation

Leveraging AI and technology to create personalized experiences that truly understand and support our users' needs.

Empowerment

Building confidence, skills, and networks that enable women to create sustainable businesses and transform their communities.

Our Mission

To empower Ethiopian women entrepreneurs by providing a comprehensive ecosystem of sisterhood support, AI-powered mentorship, skill exchange opportunities, and access to resources tailored to their unique cultural and economic context.

We aim to reduce the isolation faced by solo entrepreneurs and create pathways to sustainable business success through community, technology, and targeted support.

Our Vision

A thriving ecosystem where Ethiopian women entrepreneurs have equal access to resources, mentorship, and opportunities, creating a new generation of successful business leaders who transform their communities and inspire future generations.

We envision Ethiopia as a hub of women-led innovation, where sisterhood support systems enable sustainable economic growth and social transformation.
